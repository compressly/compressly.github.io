Before opening Compressly

A quick note about operating system warnings.

Compressly is distributed directly through GitHub. Small, independently distributed apps can trigger reputation-based warnings even when the files are legitimate.

Windows SmartScreen

If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC,” choose More info, confirm the publisher information shown by your download, then choose Run anyway if you trust the file.

Never disable real-time protection globally just to install an application.

macOS Gatekeeper

If you see a message saying Apple could not verify that Compressly is free of malware, follow these steps to allow the app to run:

  1. Open System Settings and go to Privacy & Security.
  2. In the Security section, find the message saying that Compressly was blocked to protect your Mac.
  3. Click Open Anyway. If another warning appears, click Open Anyway again.
  4. Enter your administrator password if macOS asks you to confirm.
  5. Open Compressly again.
