Two workflows

Choose certainty or let Compressly decide.

Target Size

Fit a known limit

Use the standard workflow when an upload, message, or platform has a hard maximum size.

  • Exact target-size presets
  • Custom FPS and resolution
  • Priority controls for video or audio
  • Automatic retries when output is oversized
Smart Compression

Keep it looking right

Use Smart mode when your goal is meaningful savings without micromanaging bitrate or inventing a target.

  • Fidelity-focused quality profiles
  • Resolution and FPS retention
  • Per-file media decisions
  • Sampled visual comparison after processing
Still simple

From media to smaller media in three steps.

Add your files

Select or drag in images, videos, and audio. There is no upload and no arbitrary file-size ceiling.

Choose your approach

Set an exact target or enter Smart Compression and choose the qualities you want preserved.

Review real results

Outputs stay next to your originals. Open Details or the Smart dashboard to understand what changed.
