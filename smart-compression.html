How it works

Simple choices, media-aware decisions.

Smart Compression does not apply one blunt recipe to every file. Images, video, and audio each receive a strategy suited to their format.

Analyze the source

Compressly reads dimensions, frame rate, codecs, duration, audio, and the selected quality profile before encoding.

Protect your priorities

Retention controls keep original resolution, motion cadence, or audio handling when those qualities matter more than maximum savings.

Explain the outcome

The completion dashboard reports actual bytes saved and sampled visual similarity, with technical audio results instead of a fake quality percentage.

Quality profiles

Choose the balance, not the bitrate

Profiles set the overall intent while keeping the interface approachable.

  • Maximum fidelity for near-original presentation
  • Balanced for everyday optimization
  • Smaller file for stronger practical savings
Retention controls

Keep what the source does well

Toggle only the attributes you want Compressly to protect.

  • Retain original resolution
  • Retain original frame rate
  • Favor source audio quality
  • Remove unnecessary metadata separately

Honest comparison

Quality is measured after compression.

For images and videos, Compressly uses FFmpeg’s SSIM filter to compare decoded source and output samples on a normalized canvas. The score describes sampled structural similarity, not subjective perfection.

1
Bounded samplingImages use one decoded frame. Videos use up to five frames taken across the timeline.
2
Real before and afterThe completed output is compared against the actual source, after encoding has finished.
3
No invented audio scoreAudio results report whether it was copied or re-encoded, plus codec and bitrate.
